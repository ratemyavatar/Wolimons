Writing

A few habits that keep the pages sounding like one site.

  • Values are R$ 10,000 — thousands separated, currency in front.
  • Dates are plain: 2026-08-20, or "2 minutes ago" for anything recent.
  • Say what happened, not what the code did: "Saved." beats "Mutation committed".
  • Empty lists explain themselves: "Nobody has commented yet", never a blank box.
